CWS POS - Quick Guide
=====================

GETTING STARTED
1. Open CWS POS from the desktop shortcut or Start menu.
2. Log in with the account given to you.
3. If asked, open the till and enter the opening cash amount.

MAKING A SALE
1. Scan a barcode, or search / tap a product to add it.
2. Use + / - to change quantity, x to remove an item.
3. Press F2 (or the Charge button) to pay.
4. Choose Cash, M-Pesa, Airtel or Card and follow the prompts.
5. Print the receipt, then start a new sale.

END OF SHIFT
- X Report: shows sales so far, does not close the till.
- Close Till: enter closing cash to print the Z Report.

NO INTERNET
- Cash sales still work and are saved on the device.
- They sync automatically when the connection returns.

SHORTCUTS
F2 Charge | F4 Hold order | Esc Close dialog

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- "VCRUNTIME140.dll / MSVCP140.dll not found":
  install the Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64):
  https://aka.ms/vs/17/release/vc_redist.x64.exe
- "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
- Cannot log in or load products: check your internet connection.
- Receipt not printing: check paper, power and USB cable, then select
  the printer in Settings and press Test Print.
